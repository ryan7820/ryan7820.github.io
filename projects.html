Aircraft Design · MATLAB · Stability

Balsa Glider: Aerodynamic Design & Stability

Jan–May 2026

Designed and iterated on a glider configuration using an SG6043 wing and NACA 0012 tail. Compared center-of-gravity placements and evaluated longitudinal static stability, predicting a 7.34° trim angle and a maximum lift-to-drag ratio of 14.8.

Class project; source code private to respect academic integrity.
